       fused-silica window -> LSP chamber entrance. No plasma, nozzle, thrust or trajectory modelling.

cycle are UNRESOLVED. Results below are NOT a claim of sustained 30 kW propulsion operation, and the
selected components have NOT been validated as a complete propulsion laser system.
